EVALUATION NOTES

What these metrics mean

Local demo metrics use a chronological holdout from the supplied training data. The model candidates are selected by holdout RMSE, then refit on all official training rows for demonstration predictions.

These are not QuickML metrics and do not predict hidden-set competition accuracy. QuickML status is not confirmed until an inference request succeeds.

ⓘAll model output is a point estimate. Forecast uncertainty is not calibrated or quantified.
